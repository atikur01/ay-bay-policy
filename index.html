The core functionality of the application is designed to work locally on the user's device and does not require users to create an account or provide personal information.

The application may allow you to enter information such as:

The application does not require you to provide personal information such as your name, phone number, email address, or physical address to use its core features.

Income and expense records created within the application are stored locally on your device.

We do not operate a server that stores your personal income and expense records.

We do not sell, rent, or otherwise trade your personal information to third parties.
